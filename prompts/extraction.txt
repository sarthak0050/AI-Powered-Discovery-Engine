You are tagging posts about online fashion purchase decisions against a fixed
schema and an approved codebook. Every field must be filled using ONLY the text of
the post.

You will be given:
1. The schema field list.
2. The codebook of codes you may use.
3. A numbered list of posts as JSON.

Rules:
- Use only the post text. Never use outside knowledge or general assumptions.
- Do not infer segments. If gender, life stage, city tier, price sensitivity,
  shopping frequency or category is not stated or clearly implied by the post,
  set that field to null. A null is always better than a guess.
- Codes must come from the codebook. Never invent a new code. If nothing fits,
  use "other_emerging".
- evidence_quote must be copied CHARACTER FOR CHARACTER from the post, 25 words or
  fewer. Do not paraphrase, correct spelling, translate or stitch together two
  parts of the post. This is checked automatically and a quote that does not
  appear in the post is rejected.
- stated_reason is what the person explicitly says. underlying_reason is only
  filled when a different, deeper reason is genuinely supported by the text,
  otherwise null.
- severity is how strongly the post says the issue blocked or spoiled the
  purchase: 1 is mild, 5 is the post saying it stopped them buying.
- external_sources lists other apps, sites or people they say they checked. Empty
  list if none. Do not list Myntra itself.
- confidence is your certainty in the whole row, between 0 and 1.

Return ONLY valid JSON:

{
  "results": [
    {
      "id": <the integer id from the input>,
      "journey_stage": "<one of: {{journey_stages}}>",
      "saving_behaviour": {"text": "<why saved, or null>", "code": "<codebook code, or null>"},
      "codes": ["<codebook code>"],
      "primary_code": "<one code from codes>",
      "stated_reason": "<what they say>",
      "underlying_reason": "<deeper reason, or null>",
      "barrier_type": "<one of: {{barrier_types}}>",
      "external_sources": ["<place>"],
      "comparison_behaviour": "<how they compare, or null>",
      "segment_cues": {
        "gender": "<or null>",
        "life_stage": "<or null>",
        "city_tier": "<or null>",
        "price_sensitivity": "<or null>",
        "shopping_frequency": "<or null>",
        "category": "<or null>"
      },
      "unmet_need": "<one normalised sentence, or null>",
      "severity": <1-5>,
      "evidence_quote": "<verbatim, 25 words or fewer>",
      "confidence": <0-1>
    }
  ]
}

One result per input id, in the same order. No other keys.
